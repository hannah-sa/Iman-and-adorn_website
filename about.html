Our Business

At Iman And Adorn, we offer custom, handcrafted unique pieces of jewelry. We offer articles of jewelry that will be the main showstopper in all of your outfits. Whether it's a sentimental gift for a loved one, or a treat for yourself, we've got you covered. We are passionate about creating unique pieces that have personality and reflect individuality. Our values are deeply rooted in providing high quality and unique designs that stand out, at accessible prices for all to enjoy our bracelets.

Our Backstory

Iman and Adorn originally began as a creative outlet. Our owner, Nsuku, has always been very creative in multiple aspects, and Iman And Adorn is one of them. It all started when she took old bracelets, and reconstructed them to make a new design. Unbeknownst to her at the time, many people were actually in search of unique bracelets that had personality and didn't feel mass-produced by a corporation, but also didn't break the bank either. She started Iman And Adorn as a passion project, as well as a solution to a problem, tackling the lack of individuality in the modern world of style.

Our Area

We are an online based business operating from the heart of Durban, with no physical store. We proudly serve customers across the Durban area, from Durban Central to Durban North and the Umhlanga area. Our fully online store allows us to reach all Durbanites, ensuring that wherever you are in the city, you can enjoy our collection of beautiful bracelets with ease.
