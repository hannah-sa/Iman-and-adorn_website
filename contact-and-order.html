How to order

Step 1

1. Browse our collection

Explore our Products Page or our Instagram feed for the bracelet/s you want.

Step 2

2. Check availability

Ensure the item is in stock by contacting us below.

Step 3

3. Send us a message

Contact us with the following details: item/s name/s, quantity and/or description and your preferred colour or size. Fill out the Contact & Order form below. We will contact you as soon as possible after that.

Step 4

4. Send payment

Once the process of ordering your bracelet is complete, we will send you our banking details and total the amount. We deliver through PUDO for R60 and pickup depends on your location within Durban.
